My Narrative

I am an Applied AI Engineer with 11+ years of combined experience in production software control engineering and applied AI systems. I specialize in the critical layer where models meet production code: LLM response evaluation, regression testing, RAG architecture, and deployment pipeline reliability.

Earlier in my career, I spent over 6+ years at Jetec Corporation in California as a Senior Software Control Engineer. I maintained and modernized a 40K+ LOC real-time C++/C# control codebase for Fortune 500 defense contractors (Lockheed Martin, Northrop Grumman, Raytheon). Delivering zero-failure software under ITAR and MIL-STD compliance taught me a rigorous debugging discipline and system safety mindset that I now apply to scaling unpredictable LLM architectures.

Since 2022, I have consulted on high-performance computing, vector database architectures, and RLHF data evaluation pipelines. As a freelance AI evaluator for Outlier and Scale AI, I have completed 450+ tasks reviewing model code and multi-turn reasoning logic, helping align LLMs for correctness and safety.
